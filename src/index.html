    Route sources & accuracy

    The driving route follows NASA's published Curiosity rover path, simplified to approximately 15 m to make its bends practical to follow in the game. It is a snapshot, not a live location. Named stops are approximate matches to the earlier screenshot estimates. Short gaps in the source are connected for gameplay.

    The map loads NASA's Gale Crater imagery where available and applies an illustrative tint for consistent color. Outside its coverage or offline, terrain colors are shown instead. The imagery does not change the game's 3D elevation.
