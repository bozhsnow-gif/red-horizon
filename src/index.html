    Route sources & accuracy

    The driving route follows NASA's published Curiosity rover path, simplified to approximately 15 m to make its bends practical to follow in the game. It is a snapshot, not a live location. Named stops are approximate matches to the earlier screenshot estimates. Short gaps in the source are connected for gameplay.

    The map loads NASA's Gale Crater imagery where available and applies an illustrative tint for consistent color. Outside its coverage or offline, terrain colors are shown instead. The imagery does not change the game's 3D elevation.

    Radiation & magnetic shielding

    Explore a conceptual cross-section of a habitat. The paths illustrate possible interactions; they are not calculated trajectories or measured protection.

    Charged particles can bend in a magnetic field; some high-energy particles can still penetrate.

    Neutrons are not deflected by magnetic fields. Hydrogen-rich material can slow them, and some still pass through.

    Illustrative concept, not a prediction of dose or shielding effectiveness. Field strength, geometry, particle energy and shielding coverage all matter.

    NASA-funded CREW HaT simulations found an additional 8–18% reduction in GCR equivalent dose for the material-shielded cases studied. These spacecraft simulations do not predict this habitat's protection on Mars.
