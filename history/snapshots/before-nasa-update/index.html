Map calibration & scientific sources

Terrain elevation estimated from your colour-coded map, not an original digital elevation dataset. Scale: 50 km over approximately 112 image pixels. The source crop spans 192.4 × 173.7 km. At your requested 70% scale, the game spans 134.7 × 121.6 km; all vertical relief is also multiplied by 0.7, preserving slopes and proportions. Elevation readouts and contour labels retain original reference-map values. The −4,500 to +1,500 m legend’s datum and the image projection are unspecified. Image shading, compression and a roughly 0.9 km grid limit accuracy. High-altitude views enhance shading and show 250 m contours without changing terrain heights. Compass north follows the top of the supplied map. The white landing-circle annotation is masked and filled from surrounding terrain. Latitude/longitude uses an approximate north-up equirectangular registration: the visually estimated crater center at image pixel (216, 216) is tied to the USGS center, 5.44° S, 137.70° E. Coordinates refer to the source map even though gameplay distances are scaled to 70%. They are not survey-accurate; adding the grid does not improve the underlying elevation data. The spherical calculation uses a 3,396,190 m Mars radius and positive-east longitudes.

Rocks, materials, science stops and the suggested route are illustrative, not georeferenced observations. Simulation mode caps speed at 0.04 m/s. ARES remains a fictional rover with simplified traction and suspension.
